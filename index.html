Ranking points, explained

See exactly which results make your ranking.

USTA's Combined Standings use your best 6 singles results (each with its bonus points for beating top-500 players) plus 15% of your best 6 doubles, over the last 52 weeks. AceLens shows yours exactly as USTA counts them — and when each one drops off.

  • Change a result in the calculator → watch which ones count
  • "The one to beat": the lowest counted result a new one must top
  • Weekly refresh, the day after USTA updates

    Questions

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes — every new install has a month free with everything unlocked and no card — just your name and email before the download. When the trial ends, AceLens shows a button that brings you to the request form with your machine code filled in, or email acelens.app@gmail.com — we'll get you set up. Your data stays on your PC the whole time.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Your tennis data — tournaments, results, history, notes — stays on your computer. AceLens reads public tournament pages directly from your computer, gently, and never sends that data to the seller or anyone else. What AceLens does receive: the name and email (and any optional answers) you give before downloading or when you request a code, and a once-a-day check-in from the app with its anonymous machine code, version and computer type (so trials, activations and updates work). The optional morning email is sent only to the addresses you choose. If you switch on the optional live calendar (so Google Calendar or Outlook show your tournaments), your tournament list — names, dates and places — is kept on AceLens's calendar service at a private link so your calendar can read it; switching it off deletes it.

    Do I need a USTA or UTR account?

    Not for finding tournaments, fields and national standings. UTR ratings use your own UTR sign-in, done once in a window inside AceLens. Your upcoming tournaments, match history, ranking points and match-day draws use your USTA sign-in, also done once inside AceLens. AceLens never sees your passwords and only reads.

    How accurate is the weather a month out?

    Within about 6 days it's the National Weather Service forecast at the venue. Further out it's a long-range prediction: 30 years of that date at the venue, adjusted by NOAA's official outlook for this year — a guide for planning, replaced by the real forecast automatically.

    What if I get a new computer?

    Email the new PC's machine code and you'll get a code for it. Windows updates and reinstalls don't affect your activation.

    Does it work on Mac?

    Yes — AceLens runs on Macs with Apple silicon or Intel (macOS 11 or later), with everything the Windows version has: the free trial, activation, updates inside the app. The first time you open it, macOS asks you to confirm it — see How do I install it on a Mac? below for the steps.

    How do I install it on a Mac?

    Open AceLens-mac.dmg and drag AceLens into Applications. The first time, macOS asks you to confirm it:

    • macOS 15 Sequoia or later: double-click AceLens, press Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and press Open Anyway next to AceLens.
    • macOS 14 or earlier: right-click AceLens in Applications → Open → Open.
    • If macOS says the app "is damaged": open Terminal, run xattr -cr /Applications/AceLens.app, and open it again.

    After that it opens normally and updates itself. Your free trial, activation code and machine code work exactly as on Windows — send the machine code shown on the Mac to get its activation code.

    Is AceLens part of USTA or UTR?

    No. AceLens is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the USTA, Universal Tennis (UTR), NOAA or the National Weather Service. Always check official sources before entering a tournament.
