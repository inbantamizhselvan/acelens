Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your PC. AceLens reads public tournament pages directly from your computer, gently, and never sends your data to the seller or anyone else.

Do I need a USTA or UTR account?

Not for tournaments, fields and national standings. UTR ratings use your own free UTR sign-in, done once in a window; AceLens never sees your password. Draws need a one-time USTA sign-in that's erased right after reading.

What if I get a new computer?

Email the new PC's machine code and you'll get a code for it. Windows updates and reinstalls don't affect your activation.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet: Windows 10 and 11 only.

Is AceLens part of USTA or UTR?

No. AceLens is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the USTA, Universal Tennis (UTR) or the National Weather Service. Always check official sources before entering a tournament.
